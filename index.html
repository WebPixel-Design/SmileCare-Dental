Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before your visit.

Do I need to book an appointment?

Yes, we recommend booking an appointment so we can prepare a suitable time slot for you.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Walk-ins are accepted depending on availability, but appointments are prioritised.

Is teeth whitening painful?

Most patients only experience mild sensitivity, which usually goes away after treatment.

How often should I do a dental check-up?

Most people should visit the dentist every 6 months for regular check-ups and cleaning.

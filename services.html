Service FAQ

Common questions about our treatments.

Which treatment should I choose?

You can book a dental check-up first. Our team will examine your condition and suggest the most suitable treatment.

How long does scaling and polishing take?

It usually takes around 30 to 60 minutes depending on your oral condition.

Do you provide emergency treatment?

Yes. Contact us immediately if you experience severe toothache, swelling, or broken teeth.

Not Sure Which Treatment You Need?

Contact our team and we will help you choose the right treatment based on your concern.
